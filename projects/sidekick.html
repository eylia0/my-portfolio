SideKick — From Resistance to Adoption

An overview of my Industrial Design Honours thesis. The full project is shown privately while I sort out the IP.

Wearable Construction Honours thesis Confidential
CONFIDENTIALSideKick

The question

Construction has plenty of new tech, but very little of it actually gets used on site. My thesis asked why tradies push back on new tools, and how to design a wearable they actually want to wear.

What I did

  • Research into why tradies resist new tech (psychological, cognitive, social and cost barriers), turned into design requirements
  • Research through design: round after round of prototypes, each tested against those requirements
  • Soft goods and material trials, including 3D-printed TPU, cast silicone and stitched joins
  • Electronics, sensors and Arduino firmware, built and tested to a working 1:1 prototype
  • A companion web app, plus a full AS 1100 fabrication drawing set
