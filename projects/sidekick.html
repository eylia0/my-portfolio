SideKick — From Resistance to Adoption

An overview of my Industrial Design Honours thesis. The full project is shown privately while IP protection is in progress.

Wearable Construction Honours thesis Confidential
CONFIDENTIALSideKick

The question

Construction has plenty of new technology, but very little of it gets used on site. My thesis asked why tradespeople resist new tools, and how a wearable could be designed so they actually want to use it.

What I did

  • Research into the psychological, cognitive, social and economic barriers to adoption, turned into design requirements
  • Research through design: many rounds of prototypes, each tested against those requirements
  • Soft goods and material trials, including 3D-printed TPU, cast silicone and stitched joins
  • Electronics, sensors and Arduino firmware, built and tested to a working 1:1 prototype
  • A companion web app, plus a full AS 1100 fabrication drawing set
